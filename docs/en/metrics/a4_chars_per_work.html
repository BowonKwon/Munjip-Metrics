Characters per work

Median and mean characters (text + notes) of a work, by century.

75median of the 9 century medians, characters per work
Key point

The median work has 56 characters (one seven-syllable regulated verse) in the 13th–16th centuries, then 75 in the 17th, 118 in the 18th and 157 in the 19th; the 9th century (Choe Chi-won, 168) and the 12th (80) are exceptions. The direction is the same as the falling share of poetry and the rising share of prose.

Formula
median(chars_main + chars_note) over works
Caveats
Smaller where poems dominate; genre-specific length is in the poem-length metric of series B.
