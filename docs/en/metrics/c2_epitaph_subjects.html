Subjects of epitaphs and biographies

Subject strings parsed from the titles of the author's epitaphs, tomb inscriptions and biographies, with the number of distinct strings.

19,789epitaphs and biographies by the authors
Key point

880 collections contain 19,789 epitaphs and biographies by the author; a subject string was parsed from 93.6% (17,972 distinct summed over collections, 15,440 corpus-wide). The Songsa jip (968), Songja daejeon (576) and Myeonam jip (428) have the most.

Formula
Text before 墓誌銘·墓碣銘·神道碑·墓表·行狀·諡狀 etc. is the subject (honorific prefixes 贈·故·先考 removed).
Caveats
Strings carry offices and posthumous names, so distinct counts are close to work counts. Version 1.0 does not merge persons.
Reliability card
sampledSampled precision — accuracy of title parsing or person linkage measured on a coded sample

Precision 84.5% (164/194, Wilson 95% 78.8%–88.9%) on a simple random sample of 200 (194 with a pattern match form the precision denominator); unweighted sample recall 82.7% (of 197 with a coder-found name). Codebook and all judgments on the Data page.
