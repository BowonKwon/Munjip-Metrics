Other writers

Writers other than the author who contributed to the collection (prefaces, postscripts, biographies, elegies, appendices) and their works.

17,675other writers contributing (summed over collections)
Key point

1,120 collections carry 27,709 works by writers other than the author; summed over collections that is 17,675 writers, a median of 8 per collection. The Seongjae jip (173), Hanju jip (143) and Cheogam jip (138) have the most.

Formula
Distinct writer names among works whose writer ≠ author
Caveats
Follows the writer tag; a label such as '門人' (disciples) counts as one.
