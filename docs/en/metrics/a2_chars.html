Characters per collection

Chinese characters in the text of the works, counted separately for the main text and for notes (original notes, collation and marginal notes).

182.9Mcharacters incl. 5.5M in notes
Key point

177.4 million characters of text and 5.5 million in notes (3.0%); the median collection has about 80,000. The Yeoyudang jeonseo is the longest at 4.1 million, followed by the Songja daejeon (3.7 million) and the Myeonu jip (2.9 million).

Formula
CJK code points inside <내용>; chars_main outside, chars_note inside 원주/주석 elements. Check: equals a direct recount of every <내용> in the raw XML, zero residual character references.
Caveats
Punctuation, spaces and Hangul are not counted. The ITKC transcription is the reference; characters that differ from the facsimile pages (Choi et al. 2021) are not corrected.
