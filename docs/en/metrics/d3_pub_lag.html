Authors and publicationyears

Publication lag

Median and quartiles of years from the author's death to the printing of the source edition, by century.

66years from death to printing (median)
Key point

1,038 collections have a printing year and 1,026 also a death year to measure the lag. The median lag from death to printing is 66 years (quartiles 24–177): 175 years for authors born in the 15th century, 128 (16th), 59 (17th), 39 (18th), 15 (19th).

Formula
median(publication year − death year), collections with a publication year only
Caveats
The bibliographic year is that of the edition reproduced in the series, not necessarily the first printing; collections without a year are excluded.
External check
Set against Kwak (2026): 'about a century'.
