Works per collection

Number of works in a collection — the ITKC's final unit (one poem, one letter, one epitaph), split into the author's own works and works by others (prefaces, postscripts, biographies, appendices).

637,203works · 1,249 collections
Key point

637,203 works in 1,249 collections; the median collection has 339. The largest are the Songja daejeon (9,434), the Yeoyudang jeonseo (8,100) and the Myeonu jip (5,688). 95.7% of all works are by the collection's own author.

Formula
Count of type="최종정보" elements; own work = the work's writer equals the collection's author.
Caveats
A 'work' is a unit cut by the ITKC editors: a poem cycle '… 十首' may be one work or ten. Read together with the character counts.

exactExact count — read straight from the source XML
