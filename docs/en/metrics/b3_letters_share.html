Share of letters

Share of own works that are letters (書簡類), by century.

13.4%of own works are letters
Key point

Letters are 13.4% of own works: 0.3–2.5% in the 12th–15th centuries, then 5.7% (16th), 15.0% (17th), 15.1% (18th) and 30.2% (19th); the 9th century (Choe Chi-won) stands at 32.9%. The direction matches the rising trend Kwak (2026) reports.

Formula
Letter works / own works
Caveats
Only letters selected for the printed collection, not all letters written.
Reliability card
exactExact count — read straight from the source XML

Classification quality: a stratified sample of 300 own works (major class × century) was blind-coded from titles; 97.3% agree with the ITKC major class (Wilson 95% 94.8%–98.6%; 3 undecided counted as disagreement; a second coder re-coded 30 of them blind with 97% agreement). Known block error: 284 works of 湖陰雜稿 (ITKC_MO_0126A) filed as 冊文類 carry poem titles; they are left as classified and noted here.
