Share of poetry

Share of the author's own works classified as poetry (詩類), by works and by characters. The site's headline metric.

62.0%of own works are poetry (by works, all centuries)
Key point

62.0% of the authors' own 609,494 works are poems. By century the share falls from 88% (14th–15th) to 75% (16th), 62% (17th), 51% (18th) and 42% (19th). By characters poetry is 18.8%: many works, few characters. The direction matches Kwak (2026): poetry largest, declining.

Formula
Poetry works / own works (per century); poetry characters / own characters
Caveats
Uses the ITKC classification as given (rhapsodies and lyrics are subclasses of 詩類). Where a cycle of ten poems is one work, the work-based share falls and the character-based share does not.
External check
Direction compared with Kwak (2026): poetry largest, gradual decline, 20th-century rebound.
