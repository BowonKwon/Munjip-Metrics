Share of poetry

Share of the author's own works classified as poetry (詩類), by works and by characters. The site's headline metric.

62.0%of own works are poetry (by works, all centuries)
Key point

62.0% of the authors' own 609,494 works are poems. By century the share falls from 88% (14th–15th) to 75% (16th), 62% (17th), 51% (18th) and 42% (19th). By characters poetry is 18.8%: many works, few characters. The direction matches Kwak (2026): poetry largest, declining.

Formula
Poetry works / own works (per century); poetry characters / own characters
Caveats
Uses the ITKC classification as given (rhapsodies and lyrics are subclasses of 詩類). Where a cycle of ten poems is one work, the work-based share falls and the character-based share does not.
External check
Direction compared with Kwak (2026): poetry largest, gradual decline, 20th-century rebound.
Reliability card
exactExact count — read straight from the source XML

Direction compared with Kwak (2026): poetry largest, gradual decline, 20th-century rebound.

Classification quality: a stratified sample of 300 own works (major class × century) was blind-coded from titles; 97.3% agree with the ITKC major class (Wilson 95% 94.8%–98.6%; 3 undecided counted as disagreement; a second coder re-coded 30 of them blind with 97% agreement). Known block error: 284 works of 湖陰雜稿 (ITKC_MO_0126A) filed as 冊文類 carry poem titles; they are left as classified and noted here.

Reading the by-century charts: left to right is the century of the author's birth, and each point is computed over all the collections of authors born in that century. No collection has an author born in the 10th century or 11th century, so the line breaks there. The points for the 9th century (2 collections), 12th century (2 collections), 13th century (7 collections) rest on only a few collections.
