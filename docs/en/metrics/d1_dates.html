Date completeness and lifespan

Share of authors with both birth and death years, and the median lifespan of those who have them, by century.

98.4%of authors have both birth and death years
Key point

Of 1,248 authors, 1,240 have a birth year, 1,231 a death year and 1,228 (98.4%) both. Median lifespan is 65.5 years, from 57.5 (15th century) to 70 (18th). Of the 1,077 authors matched to the AKS person database (by name and birth year, or name and pen name) with a comparable death year, 1,035 (96.1%) agree.
