Share of court documents

Share of own works in the court-document class (公車類: memorials, examination answers, royal edicts), by century.

5.6%of own works are court documents
Key point

Court documents (memorials, examination answers, edicts) are 5.6% of own works. Apart from the 9th century (Choe Chi-won, 42%), the share peaks in the 16th (5.8%) and 17th (7.7%) centuries and falls to 2.4% in the 19th.

Formula
Court-document works / own works
Caveats
A genre only office-holders produce; read with the author's career.
Reliability card
exactExact count — read straight from the source XML

Classification quality: a stratified sample of 300 own works (major class × century) was blind-coded from titles; 97.3% agree with the ITKC major class (Wilson 95% 94.8%–98.6%; 3 undecided counted as disagreement; a second coder re-coded 30 of them blind with 97% agreement). Known block error: 284 works of ITKC_MO_0126A filed as 冊文類 carry poem titles; they are left as classified and noted here.
