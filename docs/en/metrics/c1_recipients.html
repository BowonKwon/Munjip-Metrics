Letter addressees and concentration

Distinct addressee strings parsed from the titles of the author's letters, the share of the top one and top three, and a Herfindahl index.

34,980addressee strings across 895 collections
Key point

In the 895 collections with letters, 93.7% of the 81,842 letters name an addressee in the title, giving 34,980 addressee strings. A collection's median is 14 distinct addressees, with the top three taking 41% of its letters. The Ganjae jip (1,290), Myeonu jip (1,033) and Songja daejeon (801) have the most.

Formula
If the title begins with 答·與·上·寄·復·報·奉·呈·贈·簡·謝, the addressee is the text up to 書·簡·問目·別紙·狀 etc.; distinct = distinct strings.
Caveats
String level: '退溪先生', '李景浩' and '李滉' are three addressees. Parsing precision was measured on a sample (reliability card).
