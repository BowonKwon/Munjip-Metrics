Seven-class genre mix

How the author's own works divide among the seven major classes (poetry, letters, prefaces and postscripts, biographies, memorials, elegies, miscellany), by century.

13.4%share of the second-largest class, letters
Key point

Of own works: poetry 62.0%, letters 13.4%, miscellany 9.1%, court documents 5.6%, biographies 3.7%, elegies 3.2%, prefaces 3.0%. By characters the order changes: miscellany 25.2%, poetry 18.8%, letters 17.8%, biographies 16.9%. Among minor classes poems (374,153), letters (81,442), 'other' within miscellany (26,505) and memorials (26,386) lead.

Formula
Works per major class / own works; characters likewise
Caveats
Miscellany (雜著類) is large: essays, records, inscriptions and 'other' all fall there. A separate table gives the minor classes.
Reliability card
exactExact count — read straight from the source XML

Classification quality: a stratified sample of 300 own works (major class × century) was blind-coded from titles; 97.3% agree with the ITKC major class (Wilson 95% 94.8%–98.6%; 3 undecided counted as disagreement; a second coder re-coded 30 of them blind with 97% agreement). Known block error: 284 works of 湖陰雜稿 (ITKC_MO_0126A) filed as 冊文類 carry poem titles; they are left as classified and noted here.
